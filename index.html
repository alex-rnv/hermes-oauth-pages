Hermes Personal Assistant

Hermes Personal Assistant is a private, single-user automation tool that helps its owner manage their own Google Workspace data — email, calendar, Drive files, Sheets, and Docs — through the official Google APIs.

This application is operated by an individual for personal use only. It is not offered as a public product or service, and it does not collect, process, or store data belonging to anyone other than its owner.
